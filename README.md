## Projects

### [Latency-Sensitive Offloading in Digital-Twin-Enabled IoV](https://github.com/VinamraOberoi/PSO-algorithm-for-DTIoV)
Final-year research project. Particle Swarm Optimization for task offloading in an
Intelligent Internet of Vehicles network, simulated over the real road geography of
Delhi: 1,000 vehicles choosing between local, roadside-unit, base-station and cloud
execution, optimised against a joint latency/throughput objective, and benchmarked
against differential evolution, ant colony optimisation and a genetic algorithm.
`Python` · `NumPy` · `GeoPandas` · `Folium` · `Optimisation`

### [Bill Analyzer](https://github.com/VinamraOberoi/bill-analyzer)
Hackathon build. Reads a photograph of a receipt — uploaded or captured live from a
webcam — extracts the text with OCR, classifies the vendor category, parses the total,
and logs it to CSV for expense tracking.
`Python` · `OpenCV` · `Tesseract OCR` · `Tkinter`

### Fire and smoke detection
CNN detecting fire and smoke in live video feeds, with data preprocessing, augmentation
and Keras-Tuner hyperparameter search to push detection accuracy.
`Python` · `TensorFlow` · `Keras` · `OpenCV`

### Seatbelt detection
Computer vision classifier for seatbelt compliance in vehicles, built for a Vehant
Technologies competition. Tuned CNN architectures and hyperparameters to cut false
positives.
`Python` · `TensorFlow` · `Keras` · `Image classification`

### Smart GPS tag
Arduino-based asset tracker for real-time location monitoring, with YOLOv5 object
detection layered on top of the tracking.
`Python` · `YOLOv5` · `C++` · `Arduino` · `Embedded systems`
